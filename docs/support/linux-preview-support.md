# Fedora Linux Support Status

Fedora 43 x86_64 on KDE Plasma under Wayland is the supported Linux target.
Support covers the signed direct-download RPM, KDE Wallet's Secret Service
bridge, the session notification service, and `hcb-reminderd`. Other Fedora
editions, distributions, desktop environments, and architectures are outside
the support contract until separately validated.

Use the [Fedora 43 KDE Wayland acceptance checklist](../testing/manual-fedora43-kde-wayland.md)
when reporting or reproducing a platform issue. Include Fedora/KDE versions and
whether the Secret Service and desktop notification services are available; do
not include OAuth tokens, client IDs, account names, or local database files.
