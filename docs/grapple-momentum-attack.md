# Grapple-momentum attack

Grapple contacts qualify at 18 planar speed and use a 2.6-unit hit range. A qualifying impact applies a forward impulse from 8 to 18 as momentum rises to 34.

Dependencies: `SpeedPlayer`, `TraversalMelee`, `GrappleImpact`, and `WildlifeAgent`. Work is constant-time after a qualifying contact.

Out of scope: normal melee, player damage, wildlife health, animation, rewards, and grapple physics changes.
