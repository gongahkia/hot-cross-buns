Privacy — Hot Cross Buns public web app

The public Hot Cross Buns web build is a static, direct Google client. Hot Cross Buns does not operate an account service, database, refresh-token service, sync mirror, analytics service, or notification service for this build.

Direct connection

When you choose direct Google connection, the browser sends Google Tasks, Calendar, and optional Drive metadata requests directly to Google using your own Google Cloud Web OAuth client. Its short-lived access token stays in page memory. Browser-local IndexedDB stores the cache, settings, and pending offline mutations. Clearing site data removes that local data.

Self-hosted reliable connection

If you independently deploy the documented full stack, its operator—not Hot Cross Buns—must publish the privacy policy for that domain. That service stores an encrypted Google refresh token, an opaque session, Calendar and Task mirrors, and optional Web Push subscriptions in infrastructure controlled by its operator. Do not use a self-hosted deployment until you understand and accept that operator's policy.

Google data

Hot Cross Buns uses authorized Google data only for visible task, note, calendar, sync, search, reminder, and attachment-picker features. It does not request Gmail, Contacts, or Photos access. The public direct build does not sell, advertise with, broker, or train models on Google user data because it has no HCB-operated data service.
